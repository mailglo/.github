# MailGlo

Keep your business email trusted.

MailGlo manages SPF and DMARC, monitors senders, and helps you fix authentication problems. These settings help inboxes check that mail in your name really comes from you. See which services send email in your name and where their authentication fails. You approve new senders and policy changes.

Manage your own domains or your clients' email settings. Use the dashboard, connect an AI assistant, or work from your terminal.

- **Check a domain** without an account: https://mailglo.com/check/
- **Start free:** one domain is free. Plans: https://mailglo.com/pricing/
- **Read the guides:** https://mailglo.com/docs/

## Use it from your own tools

- [`mailglo/cli`](https://github.com/mailglo/cli): the open-source command-line tool. Install it with `go install github.com/mailglo/cli/cmd/mailglo@latest`. Guide: https://mailglo.com/docs/cli/
- HTTP API, for scripts, with your permissions: https://mailglo.com/docs/api/
- MCP server at `https://mailglo.com/mcp`, so AI assistants can read your domains and prepare changes: https://mailglo.com/docs/mcp/
